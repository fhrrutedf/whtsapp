# OmniDesk Enterprise Growth & RevOps Skills

Strategic executive and revenue intelligence skills adapted from enterprise SaaS methodologies for OmniDesk Helpdesk.

## 1. Customer Success Manager (`customer_success_manager`)
- **Health Score**: Deterministic 0-100 scoring based on usage frequency (30%), active seats (25%), open tickets (25%), and sentiment (20%).
- **Churn Risk Prediction**: Categorizes accounts into `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`, `CRITICAL` risk tiers with prescriptive retention scripts.
- **Expansion Opportunity**: Identifies accounts ready for tier upgrades (Pro/Enterprise) or additional WhatsApp channel licenses.

## 2. Revenue Operations (`revenue_operations`)
- **Sales Velocity**: Computes `(Opportunities * Avg Value * Win Rate) / Sales Cycle Days` to project daily pipeline revenue.
- **Pipeline Coverage Ratio**: Monitors coverage against target quotas (3-4x healthy benchmark).
- **Deal Aging Auditing**: Automatically identifies and alerts on deals stalled in negotiation over 2x the normal cycle.
- **Forecast Accuracy (MAPE)**: Tracks predictability of revenue forecasts.

## 3. Pre-Sales Engineer (`sales_engineer`)
- **Competitive Matrix**: Real-time differentiation arguments against competitors (ManyChat, WATI, Respond.io, Zendesk).
- **POC Readiness**: Technical feasibility scoring and demo environments tailored to Arabic voice notes and Baileys anti-ban.

## 4. Business Growth Router (`business_growth_router`)
- **Strategic Dispatcher**: Classifies customer intent and delegates to the appropriate skill (CSM, Closer, Sales Engineer, or RevOps).
